---
title: Prices and valuation
description: How Fabriq prices tokens, values events, computes FDV and liquidity, and picks base and quote.
---

## Token prices

Fabriq values every swap it indexes. Each token's price comes from the trades that link it, pool by pool, to tokens with a known USD value such as USD stablecoins and SOL. No third-party price feed is used for ordinary tokens.

- `price_usd` is the token's price after its **latest** swap on any indexed DEX, however long ago that was.
- Each price has an estimated error. A price within **10%** is reliable.
- A token with no reliable price (a brand-new token, or one traded only in very thin pools) shows its latest price of any quality, with `price_reliable: false`. Show such prices as estimates.

Reliable prices only are used for stats that rank or score pools (price changes, APR, scores, total liquidity). A single dust trade after a rug can price a token millions of times too high, and these stats ignore it.

## Event values

Swaps, deposits, withdrawals and claims are valued **at the time they happened**, in USD and in SOL. A `Money` object carries both:

```json
"value": { "usd": 2001.25, "sol": 12.5 }
```

PnL adds up these historical values, so a position's PnL in SOL can differ in sign from its PnL in USD.

## FDV

`fdv_usd` = `price_usd` × the mint's current supply, in whole tokens. Supply is read from the token's mint account, so burned tokens are already gone. For Solana tokens this is how GMGN computes both market cap and FDV.

SOL is the exception: its supply is SOL's native total supply, since the wrapped SOL mint holds only the wrapped part.

When the price is an estimate, so is the FDV (`price_reliable: false`).

## Pool liquidity

`liquidity_usd` is the pool's two reserves valued in USD at its latest on-chain change. For Raydium AMM v4 and CPMM, reserves exclude the fees their vaults hold until they are collected.

## Token total liquidity

`total_liquidity_usd` on a token adds up every pool that holds it, both sides, at current prices:

```text
pool value  = min(X, 10 · Y) + min(Y, 10 · X)     X, Y: each side's reserve × its token's price
total       = Σ pool values over every pool holding the token
```

Each side counts at most 10 times the other. Without this cap, pools pairing USDC with worthless tokens would add billions of dollars of "liquidity" nobody can trade against. A side without a reliable price counts as 0.

## Base and quote

Every pool is shown as a **base** token traded against a **quote** token. Each side's on-chain position (`side: "x"` or `"y"`) is kept. The quote is, in order of preference:

1. A USD stablecoin (USDC, USDT, USD1, USDG). Between two stablecoins, the older one.
2. SOL.
3. Between two other tokens, the one Jupiter verifies, when only one of them is verified.
4. Otherwise, the older token.

A token's age is the earlier of its creation time and its first pool's creation time. Buys, sells and price changes are all from the base token's point of view.

## Categories

`category` comes from a curated catalog of major tokens and tokenized assets:

| `category` | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| `bluechips` | SOL, wrapped and bridged BTC and ETH, and other majors |
| `stablecoin` | USD and other fiat stablecoins |
| `equity` | Tokenized stocks |
| `commodity` | Tokenized commodities |
| `etf` | Tokenized funds |
| `rwa` | Other real-world assets |
| `lst` | Liquid staking tokens |

Tokens the catalog doesn't curate (every memecoin, but also tokens like BONK and JUP) have `category: null`.
