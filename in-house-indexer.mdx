Fabriq's in-house indexer reads Solana activity and organizes pool, position, and transaction data into a shared model. That lets you compare protocols and follow a wallet's LP activity across them.

## What the indexer powers

- [Trending Pools](/trending-pools) streams live metrics for Meteora DLMM and DAMM V2 pools.
- [Portfolio](/portfolio#supported-protocols) tracks positions and wallet activity across a broader set of programs, including concentrated-liquidity and constant-product pools.
- [Analytics](/analytics#program-filters) aggregates liquidity movement, claimed fees, participating wallets, and position activity across the programs offered in its filter.

Program coverage depends on the view. Position management also depends on the pool type and the position's available actions.

## Data freshness

The indexer status indicator in the app footer shows the age of the latest indexed data. Hover over it to see the delay.

| Status | Age of indexed data |
| --- | --- |
| Live | 15 seconds or less |
| Slow | More than 15 seconds, up to 5 minutes |
| Late | More than 5 minutes |
| Connecting | Waiting for an indexed timestamp |
