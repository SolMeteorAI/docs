Fabriq brings pool discovery, market analytics, and liquidity position management into one app for Solana liquidity providers (LPs). Its [in-house indexer](/in-house-indexer) tracks on-chain pool activity and positions across supported programs.

You can browse pools, explore market activity, and look up public wallet addresses without connecting a wallet. Connect the wallet that owns a position or token account when you're ready to manage it.
