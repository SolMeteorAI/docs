Fabriq shows reclaimable amounts for the connected wallet in the app header when available. **Portfolio → Balances** also lists eligible empty accounts and Pump.fun cashback for the selected wallet.

| Claim | What you receive |
| --- | --- |
| Empty token accounts | SOL rent recovered by closing eligible accounts with a zero token balance. |
| Excess token-account rent | SOL above the required rent-exempt balance, recovered while keeping the token account open. |
| Pump.fun cashback | Available cashback in SOL, USDC, or both, depending on the wallet's activity. |

## How to claim

1. Connect the wallet that owns the accounts or cashback.
2. Open the claim summary in the header and review the amount for the claim you want to make. For empty accounts or cashback, you can also use the corresponding row in **Portfolio → Balances**.
3. Select **Claim** and approve the transaction in your wallet. The proceeds return to that wallet after confirmation.

Fabriq's standard reclaim fee is **10%** of the recovered rent or cashback. The claim summary displays the amount after the Fabriq fee.

If an account still holds tokens, see [Balances Cleanup](/balances-cleanup) for sell and close options.
