---
title: Quickstart
description: "Find active pools, look up a wallet, and connect to manage your positions"
---

Start with a pool search or a public Solana wallet address. You can explore both without connecting a wallet.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find a pool">
    Open [Trending](https://fabriq.trade/trending). Use token search to see pools for a token, or open **Settings → Filters** to narrow the table by DEX, pool name, liquidity, volume, or fees. See [Trending Pools](/trending-pools) for the available views.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Look up a wallet">
    Open [Portfolio](https://fabriq.trade/portfolio-beta) and enter a public Solana wallet address. Use **Overview** for performance, then open **Active Positions**, **Closed Positions**, **Transactions**, or **Balances** for details. You can save wallets and [combine them into groups](/portfolio#wallet-management).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect to manage your positions">
    Select **Log In** and connect the wallet that owns the position. In **Active Positions**, open the position's action menu and review the available claim or close options before signing. See [Position Actions](/portfolio#position-actions) for protocol support.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Use [Analytics](/analytics) to compare activity across protocols, or [Rewards](/rewards) to look up Meteora rewards for a verified wallet.
