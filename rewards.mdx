[Rewards](https://fabriq.trade/rewards) shows Meteora referred-user, referral, and staking rewards, plus the Fabriq bonus for eligible wallets.

## View your Meteora rewards

1. Open **Rewards** and select **Connect wallet**.
2. Select **Verify Wallet** and sign Meteora's wallet-ownership message to load your private reward history.
3. In **Reward details**, choose **All time** or an individual reward cycle. Switch the chart between **Daily** and **Cumulative** views. Chart dates use Australia/Brisbane time (UTC+10).

## Claim the Fabriq bonus

Wallets with **FABRIQ** as their Meteora referral can earn a 100% bonus on eligible referred-user rewards. After verifying your wallet, check the **Referral** card. If the wallet has no referral code, select **Get Rewards** to link it to **FABRIQ**.

1. Open **Fabriq bonus** to review the current cycle and payment history. A completed cycle becomes claimable once rewards are finalized.
2. Select **Claim all** when a USDC balance is available. Review the included cycles and the receiving wallet.
3. Select **Sign and claim**. Track the request in the cycle history and use the payout transaction link once payment is sent.

Unclaimed Fabriq bonuses remain available after the cycle ends.
