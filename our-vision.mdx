---
title: Our Vision
description: "A unified view of liquidity provision across Solana"
---

Liquidity providers often hold positions across several DEXs. Evaluating where to deploy capital means looking at pool structure, fees, liquidity movement, and past performance together.

Fabriq's goal is to make that work possible in one place. The [in-house indexer](/in-house-indexer) gives Fabriq a common view of on-chain liquidity activity, while [Analytics](/analytics) and [Portfolio](/portfolio) let you look at that activity from the market and wallet perspectives.

Fabriq connects these views to the actions each supported protocol offers, so you can compare markets, follow positions, and manage eligible liquidity from the same app.
