---
title: Pool Alerts
description: "Create alerts from pool activity and manage delivery in Fabriq"
---

Pool alerts notify you when tracked pools match your rules. Sign in and open **Notifications** in the app header where available.

## Create an alert

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a rule">
    Open **Notifications → Alerts → New alert**, or configure your [Trending filters](/trending-pools/filters-metrics) and select **Create alert from these filters**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the criteria and cooldown">
    Give the alert a name and set at least one numeric metric range. Add pool, token, or launchpad criteria as needed. The cooldown controls how soon the same pool can trigger that alert again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview and save">
    Select **Preview** to check matches against the current snapshot. Choose any linked delivery destinations you want to use, then select **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Manage delivery

- **Inbox** shows matched alerts and notification status.
- **Alerts** lets you edit, pause, or delete rules.
- **Channels** lets you manage browser push devices, link Telegram, or connect a Discord destination. Grant notification permission when adding a browser.
