## Positions and lifecycles

Fabriq follows each LP position from its first deposit to its close. Every deposit, withdrawal, fee claim and reward claim on the way is valued in USD and SOL at the time it happened.

- A position belongs to the wallet that owns it at its latest event. When a position changes hands, the new owner gets its whole history.
- `sources` decides which positions count for a wallet: the ones it holds itself (`wallet`), the ones held through its HawkFi account (`hawkfi`), or both (the default).
- When an address is reused, each open-to-close life is its own position with its own id. See [Identifiers](/developers/conventions#identifiers).

## PnL

```text
pnl = withdrawals + fees claimed + rewards claimed + value now + unclaimed fees and rewards − deposits
```

- **Closed positions** have no value now and nothing unclaimed: their PnL is final.
- **Open positions** are valued from their on-chain accounts at request time, unclaimed fees and rewards included.
- PnL includes LP fees **and** rewards. Network fees are not included.
- Each flow keeps the USD and SOL value it had when it happened, so PnL in SOL and PnL in USD can disagree.
- A position opened before Fabriq's indexed history has `pnl: null` and `history_complete: false`.

Period endpoints (`/pnl`, `/pnl/daily`, `/pnl/by-pool`) count the positions **closed** in the period, with their whole history, including deposits made before it.

## Wins and losses

A result is **neutral** when its PnL is within 0.01% of its deposits. Win rates count wins as 1, neutrals as ½ and losses as 0:

- `positions.win_rate_pct`: share of closed positions that won.
- `days.win_rate_pct`: share of days whose closed positions won overall.
- `profit_factor`: gross profit ÷ gross loss; `null` when there are no losses.

## LP score

`GET /portfolio/{wallet}/lp-score` scores how well a wallet provides liquidity, from 0 to 100, over the positions it closed in the period. Scores are absolute: other wallets never change yours.

| Dimension | Weight | Measures |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| `profit_earned` | 30% | Positive PnL earned in the period |
| `capital_efficiency` | 20% | Profit per unit of capital at work |
| `fee_efficiency` | 20% | How much of the profit comes from fees the wallet kept after liquidity losses |
| `consistency` | 15% | The position win rate, and how evenly profit spreads across pools and days |
| `loss_control` | 15% | How deep the worst results go, relative to capital |

### What is scored

- Closed positions only, with at least $10 of known deposits, complete history and prices, and a clear owner.
- Positions in pools whose tokens fail the LP quality policy are left out: one token must be verified by Jupiter and the other must have an organic score above 30.

### Rank

`ranking` in the LP score response places the wallet among all LPs by its score over a rolling 30 days ending at the top of the hour, recomputed hourly. Only `ready` scores with enough history and profit are ranked; otherwise `ranking` is `null`.

<Note>
  LP scores describe past results. They are not a prediction of future returns.
</Note>
