description: "How Fabriq connects pool discovery, wallet tracking, and on-chain position management"
---

Fabriq indexes pool activity and LP events from Solana, then organizes the data into three views.

| View | What you can do |
| --- | --- |
| [Trending Pools](/trending-pools) | Find and compare active Meteora pools. Filter by the activity, fees, and liquidity that matter to you. |
| [Analytics](/analytics) | Compare liquidity movement, claimed fees, wallet participation, and position activity across supported programs. |
| [Portfolio](/portfolio) | Follow individual wallets or groups, review P&L and position history, and manage eligible positions. |

Pool discovery, analytics, and public wallet lookups are available before you connect. To sign an on-chain action in Portfolio, select a single wallet and connect the wallet that owns the position or token account. See [Position Actions](/portfolio#position-actions) and [SOL Reclaim](/sol-reclaim) for action availability and fees.
