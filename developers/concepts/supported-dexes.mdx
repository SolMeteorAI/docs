## Pool types

- **Concentrated**: liquidity sits in a price range of ticks. `fees.bin_step` is the pool's tick spacing.
- **Bins**: liquidity sits in discrete price bins. `fees.bin_step` is the bin step in basis points.
- **Constant product**: x·y = k. Meteora DAMM v2 pools can limit this curve to a price range.

## LP positions

- **NFT** and **position account** positions have their own address: one wallet can hold many positions in the same pool.
- **LP token** positions are a wallet's LP tokens in a pool. The API treats them as one position per wallet and pool, with the id `{owner}-{pool}`.

## Limit orders

Meteora DLMM, DLMM Pro and Raydium CLMM limit orders are indexed but not part of v1.
